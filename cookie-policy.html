Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how G-TWELV (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on info@g-twelv.com (the “Website”).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember certain information, understand how visitors use the website, and improve the overall user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Our Website may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Ensuring that the Website functions properly.
  • Maintaining Website security.
  • Remembering certain settings or preferences.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with the Website.
  • Improving the performance and functionality of the Website.

We aim to use only cookies that are reasonably necessary for the operation and improvement of our Website.

3. Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for the Website to operate correctly and securely. They may support basic functionality, security, or other essential features.

Because these cookies may be necessary for the operation of the Website, they may not always be able to be disabled through our Website.

Analytics Cookies

If enabled, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our Website, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the Website.

This information helps us improve the Website and its content.

Analytics cookies will only be used if the relevant analytics services are installed on the Website and, where required by applicable law, after obtaining the appropriate consent.

Preference or Functionality Cookies

These cookies may allow the Website to remember choices or preferences made by visitors and provide a more personalized experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features or services used on the Website may be provided by third parties and may place their own cookies or similar technologies on your device.

For example, third-party services may be used for website analytics, embedded content, maps, security, or other Website functionality.

The Company does not control the cookies used by third-party services. Their use of cookies is subject to their respective privacy policies and cookie policies.

The Website may also contain links to external websites belonging to brands or other third parties. When you leave our Website and visit an external website, that website's own cookie practices apply.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies.
  • Allow cookies only from certain websites.
  • Receive a notification before cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some websites.

6. Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

You may withdraw or change your cookie preferences where the Website provides a cookie-management or consent mechanism.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our Website, the cookies and technologies we use, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated “Last Updated” date.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically.
