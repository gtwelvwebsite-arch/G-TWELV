About G-TWELV

Incorporated in 2020, G-TWELV is a global trading powerhouse specializing in essential food products. Over the years, it has curated an impressive portfolio of brands, representing them across local, regional, and international markets.

The company’s distinctive approach lies in setting up complete independent work environment and capital for each brand; assembling a dedicated, independent workforce, complete with specialized facilities and allocated capital resources, ensuring that every brand receives personalized attention and strategic support tailored to its unique needs.

G-TWELV’s subsidiaries include AFT Trading, a specialized entity focused on end-to-end food trade logistics and international shipping. AFT Trading ensures that all brands and products under the G-TWELV umbrella are managed with precision—overseeing certification compliance, professional product handling, cold chain and dry logistics coordination, and seamless global distribution.

Our Mission

To build, develop, and scale exceptional food brands through dedicated resources, specialized expertise, and end-to-end trading capabilities. G-TWELV is committed to creating independent environments where each brand can thrive, while delivering reliable sourcing, logistics, compliance, and distribution solutions that connect quality food products with markets around the world.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized food trading and brand development group, known for transforming promising food brands into sustainable international businesses through strategic investment, operational excellence, and a truly integrated global trade network.
